Why owners switch

Built around the three things that cost restaurants money.

Stop cash leaks

Every sale belongs to a shift. At closing the drawer is counted note by note, and the app shows exactly how much is over or short. Voids and refunds need a manager PIN and go into a log no one can edit.

Serve faster

Orders go from the table to the right station in one tap: curry, wok, grill or bar. Cooks see timers, late tickets turn red, and the floor shows the moment food is ready to go out.

Know your food cost

Recipes take ingredients off stock with every bill. See the cost and profit of each dish, what is running low, and what to order from each supplier.

Features

From the first order to the last rupee.

01 · Taking orders

Menus that fit how Sri Lanka eats

Group dishes by cuisine, set spice levels, and add extras with a quantity: two boiled eggs, an extra portion of rice, a second curry. Every price updates as you go, and ready-made items like bottled drinks can skip the kitchen.

  • +Dine-in, takeaway, delivery and counter orders
  • +Courses, kitchen notes and dish photos

02 · Kitchen

A kitchen screen that keeps food moving

Tickets split automatically by station and move from new, to cooking, to ready. Each cook can filter to their own station, mains can be held until the kitchen fires them, and late tickets stand out in red.

  • +Kitchen tickets on screen, on paper, or both
  • +Spice level and add-ons printed on every ticket
  • +Void slips sent to the kitchen automatically

03 · Billing

Bills that add up, every time

Every dish and add-on is listed with its price. Apply a discount, service charge or tip, split the bill and save the customer's name and phone number. For cash the app won't accept less than the total and works out the change; for cards it records the card type and last four digits.

  • +Cash, card and mobile wallet payments
  • +Refunds with a manager PIN and a printed slip
  • +Your logo, address and VAT number on every bill

04 · Cash shifts

Close the drawer to the rupee

Open each shift with a float, record cash in and cash out with a reason, and count the drawer from Rs 5,000 notes down to Rs 1 coins. The Z report prints on the spot and is emailed to the owner, with an alert when the cash is short.

  • +No new orders until a shift is open
  • +Card payments split by Visa, Mastercard and Amex
  • +Read-only audit log of every sensitive action

05 · Stock & recipes

See the profit on every plate

Enter what goes into one portion and Virundhu & Co does the rest: ingredients come off stock with each bill, food cost updates as prices change, and the reorder list groups what's running low by supplier.

  • +Suppliers, deliveries and amounts owed
  • +Waste and stock counts with their value
  • +Warning when a dish is low on ingredients

06 · Reports

Run the restaurant from your phone

Sales by day, busy hours, top and slowest dishes, payments, staff and table turnover, for any date range. Export to Excel for your accountant, or print it as a PDF.

  • +Z report by email when every shift closes
  • +Voids and refunds by staff member
  • +Profit by dish and food cost percentage

Questions owners ask

What happens when the internet goes down?+

With the shop server, nothing changes for your staff: orders, kitchen tickets, printing and billing keep working on the shop Wi-Fi. Everything syncs to the cloud when the connection is back.

What hardware do I need?+

Any tablet or computer with a modern web browser, and a 58 mm or 80 mm thermal printer if you want printed bills and tickets. We can supply a ready-to-use bundle.

Can I keep my current menu and prices?+

Yes. We enter your full menu during setup, including spice options and add-ons, and you can change prices yourself at any time from Admin.

Does it handle VAT and service charge?+

Yes. VAT and service charge are set once and calculated on every bill, and your VAT number prints on the receipt.

How do I stop staff misusing voids and refunds?+

Voids after an item is sent, refunds and cash out all need a manager PIN, and each one is recorded in a log that cannot be edited or deleted.

What support do I get?+

Setup and staff training at your restaurant, then support on WhatsApp in Tamil, Sinhala or English.
