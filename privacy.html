Privacy policy

This policy explains how Virundhu & Co ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal data when you visit virunthu.shop, contact us, or use the Virundhu & Co restaurant POS and kitchen system (the "Service"). We follow the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 of Sri Lanka, as amended.

Two kinds of data

  • Data about our customers. When a restaurant owner or manager contacts us, signs up or pays for the Service, we decide how that data is used. For this data we are the controller.
  • Data a restaurant enters into the Service. Staff names, orders, bills and the names and phone numbers of diners belong to the restaurant. The restaurant decides what to record and why; we store and process it only to run the Service on the restaurant's behalf. For this data we act as a processor, and the restaurant is the controller.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us on WhatsApp, by email or in person: your name, business name, phone number, email address and messages.
  • Account and billing details: restaurant name, address, VAT or business registration number, the plan you choose, invoices and payment records.
  • Restaurant data in the Service: menus, prices, tables, staff names and roles, orders, kitchen tickets, bills, cash shift records, stock, suppliers and reports.
  • Diner details that a restaurant chooses to add to a bill, such as a name and phone number.
  • Card payments: the Service records only the card type and the last four digits from the card machine slip, and an optional approval code. We never collect or store full card numbers, expiry dates or security codes, and we do not process card payments ourselves.
  • Security and technical data: sign-in records, an audit log of sensitive actions (such as voids, refunds and cash movements), and server logs used to keep the Service running and secure.

Staff sign in with a PIN, which is stored only in a protected (hashed) form. This website uses no advertising or tracking cookies. The Service uses one essential cookie to keep staff signed in.

How we use it

  • To provide, set up and support the Service, including on-site setup and training.
  • To bill you, keep accounting records and meet our legal obligations.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent misuse, fix problems and make backups.
  • To send reports the restaurant has switched on, such as the Z report by email.
  • To tell you about important changes to the Service. We send marketing messages only if you agree, and you can stop them at any time.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use a restaurant's data or its diners' details for our own marketing.

Who we share it with

  • Service providers that host our servers, send email and run automations for us, under agreements that require them to protect the data and use it only to provide their services to us.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.
  • A new owner, if our business is sold or merged. They must protect your data as this policy does.

Where it is stored and for how long

Restaurants using the offline shop server keep their master copy of the data on that server in the restaurant. The cloud copy and our own records are kept on servers run by our hosting providers, which may be located outside Sri Lanka. We protect data with encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls by staff role, a read-only audit log and regular backups.

We keep restaurant data for as long as the subscription is active. When it ends, you can ask for an export within 90 days; after that we delete the restaurant's data from our servers and, within a further 90 days, from our backups. We keep invoices and payment records for as long as tax and accounting laws require.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your personal data, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a particular purpose, and you can withdraw any consent you have given. Diners and staff whose details were entered by a restaurant should contact that restaurant first; we will help the restaurant respond. We reply to requests within 21 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka.

The Service is meant for businesses and is not directed at children. If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and tell existing customers about important changes before they take effect.

Refund policy

We want you to be confident before you pay. These terms apply to subscriptions, setup and hardware bought directly from Virundhu & Co.

Free trial

No payment is taken during a free trial. If you decide not to continue, simply tell us before it ends and you will not be charged.

Monthly plans

Billed at the start of each month. Cancel any time; you keep access until the end of the month you have paid for. Partly used months are not refunded.

Yearly plans

Full refund if you cancel within 14 days of your first yearly payment. After that, cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep access until the end of the paid year.

Setup & training

Fully refundable if you cancel before we start work. Once your menu has been entered or the installation or training has taken place, the setup fee is not refundable.

Hardware

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery for a refund. Faulty items are repaired or replaced under the manufacturer's warranty.

Service problems

If the cloud service is unavailable for more than 24 hours in a row because of a fault on our side, we credit the affected days to your next invoice.

How to ask for a refund

Message us on WhatsApp or email us with your restaurant name, invoice number and the reason. We will confirm within 3 working days and pay approved refunds within 14 days, to the original payment method or by bank transfer. Bank charges, if any, are not refunded. This policy does not affect your rights under Sri Lankan consumer law.
